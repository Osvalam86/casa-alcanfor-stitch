## Brand & Style

This design system evokes the serene, architectural warmth of a contemporary Mexican hacienda caught in golden hour. Tailored for an exclusive event garden and hall in Zapopan, the brand communicates timeless heritage, bespoke hospitality, and artisanal tranquility. 

The aesthetic sits at the intersection of high-end editorial publishing and organic minimalism. It abandons tech-oriented design tropes—such as neon gradients, heavy dropshadows, and frosted glass—in favor of tactile authenticity: the texture of hand-plastered walls, natural limestone, fine linen drapery, and warm sun filtering through mature camphor foliage.

The interface should feel leisurely and curated, prioritizing spatial grace, unhurried negative space, precise typographic rhythm, and subtle architectural archways over hyper-dense layouts.

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural raw materials and landscape:
- **Canvas / Background (`#F7F2EA`)**: A soft, unbleached linen ivory that replaces sterile white, creating an immediate sense of warmth and natural stone texture.
- **Deep Sage (`#2F3E36`)**: A grounded, shaded botanical tone representing lush garden foliage and tranquil hacienda courtyards. Used for primary interactions, contrast cards, and deep evening-themed sections.
- **Antique Gold (`#7A5C30` on light surfaces, `#C9A46A` on dark surfaces)**: An artisanal metal accent reflecting warm brass fixtures and golden-hour luminescence.
- **Warm Charcoal (`#1E1A17`)**: Deep earth charcoal used for primary text and structural hairlines, offering softer, richer contrast than pure black.
- **Soft Sand Border (`#E6DFD3`)**: A subtle, low-contrast boundary line evoking quarry-cut limestone seams.

## Typography

The typography pairs the classical literary proportion of EB Garamond with the clean, balanced geometry of Manrope. 

- **EB Garamond**: Delivers high-fashion editorial sophistication for displays, titles, and emotional statements. Use italics sparingly for emphasis, foreign terms, or quotes.
- **Manrope**: Provides neutral, legible clarity across body copy, specs, capacity tables, navigation, and booking interactions.
- **Tracking & Proportion**: Headline sizes feature tight-to-natural tracking, while micro-labels, dates, and navigational markers (`label-caps`) utilize generous uppercase letter-spacing to mirror stone engravings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces editorial spaciousness, drawing inspiration from high-end architectural monographs.

- **Grid Framework**: A 12-column symmetrical fluid grid on desktop (`> 1024px`) with dynamic column spans allowing asymmetrical image-text compositions. Transitions to an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1023px`) and a 4-column layout on mobile (`< 768px`).
- **Margins & Safe Zones**: Generous outer margins frame the composition like an art catalogue page, ensuring UI elements never crowd viewport edges.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Generous vertical section spacing (`space-2xl`) allows each hacienda space (The Garden, The Master Hall, The Veranda) to breathe as a distinct story module.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established strictly through tonal layering, framing borders, and subtle structural overlaps rather than synthetic dropshadows or glassmorphism:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base level uses the linen ivory canvas (`#F7F2EA`). Elevated surfaces—such as booking modals or detail panels—use crisp bleached linen (`#FDFAF5`) or deep sage contrast (`#2F3E36`).
- **Dividers & Boundaries**: Delicate, single-pixel hairline dividers tinted in soft sand (`#E6DFD3`) or faint charcoal (`rgba(30, 26, 23, 0.12)`) articulate structure.
- **Natural Light Projection**: If soft elevation is required for floating navigation, use an imperceptible ambient shadow with a warm raw-umber tint: `0 16px 36px -12px rgba(47, 62, 54, 0.06)`, reflecting soft ambient canopy light.

## Shapes

The primary component shapes employ soft cornering (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`), echoing hand-chiseled quarry stone and dressed timber beams.

- **Architectural Archway Motifs**: For featured imagery, key gallery vignettes, and hero entryways, utilize traditional hacienda rounded top arches (`border-top-left-radius: 9999px; border-top-right-radius: 9999px; border-bottom-left-radius: 0; border-bottom-right-radius: 0`).
- **Buttons and Chips**: Styled with subtle rounding or tailored pill contours for interactive tags, maintaining a timeless, artisanal balance between organic and rectilinear structure.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Sage background (`#2F3E36`), linen ivory typography (`#F7F2EA`), minimal corner radius (`0.25rem`), `space-sm` vertical by `space-lg` horizontal padding. Hover shifts to an aged stone tone with antique gold text highlight.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Unfilled, bordered by a 1px soft sand or deep sage hairline. Warm charcoal text, hover background infill of translucent antique gold (`rgba(122, 92, 48, 0.06)`).
- **Text Action**: Underlined by a 1px antique gold baseline with subtle arrow or botanical glyph affordance.

### Cards
- **Editorial Vignette**: Ivory canvas background with an enclosing 1px sand outline (`#E6DFD3`). Imagery sits flush with an arched top or square framing, accompanied by an EB Garamond title and Manrope metadata.
- **Evening Accent Card**: Solid Deep Sage background (`#2F3E36`) with antique gold highlights (`#C9A46A`) and warm ivory text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat ivory fields framed by a crisp 1px sand baseline or complete stroke. 
- Focus state switches border color to Antique Gold (`#7A5C30`) with zero synthetic outer glow.
- Labels use small caps with generous letter spacing (`label-caps`).

### Chips & Filters
- Compact pill-shaped elements with transparent interiors and a 1px border.
- Active state: Fills with Deep Sage (`#2F3E36`) and linen text, communicating refined tactile feedback.

### Architectural Accents & Bespoke Elements
- **Botanical Hairlines**: Thin, elegant horizontal dividers punctuated by a centered small leaf, camphor branch silhouette, or diamond masonry star.
- **Capacity & Spec Badges**: Clean, tabular data layouts paired with small uppercase labels for guest counts, square footage, and shade cover.
